# Artifact scope

## Frozen scope

The selected records are a minimal, inspectable subset of the manuscript's historical compact evidence. The manuscript-source baseline is identified by a commit hash in the manifest. Earlier evidence snapshots remain independently pinned; packaging does not redate observations or imply reruns.

- **E1, Cassandra preload:** all six base/candidate paired summaries, 72 pair-record rows, and 18 construction records. The 72 ratios reuse base samples across candidates; they must not be reported as 144 independent measurements. The historical study reports 108 elapsed-time samples across nine revision/shape cells. One-large and four-small share 4,800,000 logical partitions and a digest; one-small has 1,200,000 and is not an equal-scale control. The one-large speedups and four-small null/slower outcomes are all retained.
- **E2, PostgreSQL catalog:** all nine settings and ten retained pair ratios per setting, original point/interval estimates, and illustrative 2x classifications. Two measurement batches are distinguished by opaque IDs. Fixed foreground work is not equality of whole database state. The threshold is illustrative; this is not an APOLLO end-to-end evaluation. Source-path strings have been removed.
- **E3, PostgreSQL native work:** six calibration and four within-case target cells, with timing disabled. Parent work is one visited row; introducing-revision work follows 421 + 5 times carrier count. The frozen template-match result is included. Target requests are within this known case, not prospective unseen cases. Historical elapsed-cost records were contaminated and are excluded from usable cost/performance claims.
- **E4, Cassandra native work:** 18 timing-disabled cells. Under the fixed tail-canonical construction, parent generation comparisons equal K times N; child work is one map build, N insertions, and K lookups. These heterogeneous counters are not elapsed-time speedups.
- **E5, constructor identifiability:** all nine analytical targets preserve the measured/direct-template/solver equivalence. This is an analysis of existing receipts, not a new native experiment, another engine, or proof of generic planner benefit.

The selected-claim map is an editorial aid derived from these bounded records, not an additional observation. The frozen SVG supplies the complete original Figure 3; it was not regenerated during packaging.

## Deliberate exclusions

The older broad private bundle is not included. Neither are private paths, host/cluster identifiers, account details, private URLs, credentials, runtime or environment inventories, full result prose, experimental launchers, historical next-step instructions, unpublished private material outside the selected manuscript evidence, full external raw roots, complete per-invocation receipts, builds, databases, or independent backup assertions.

E6–E8 DuckDB source/result reports are outside this minimal release candidate. Proposed receipt/state-machine source and test fixtures are outside it as well. Absence does not retract manuscript observations, but prevents treating this subset as complete support for every paper claim.

## Privacy and provenance

`artifact/manifests/provenance.json` records opaque IDs, hashes, and transformations rather than source paths or private repository links. Original numeric/Boolean cells are unchanged. Original hashes can authenticate retained author-side sources but do not make those sources accessible to readers or prove native execution. An independent content/privacy audit and rights review remain release gates.
